Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Summary: Carsheet collects no data. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your cars, entries, receipt photos and PDFs stay on your device, reminders are local notifications, and files you export go only where you send them.

1. Who we are

Carsheet is an iPhone and iPad app provided by Sergei Stasenko (“we”).

2. Data we do not collect

We don’t collect, receive or store any personal data through the app. Carsheet has no sign-in and no account, we run no server for it, and it contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or other third-party SDKs. The app doesn’t track you across apps or websites, doesn’t use the advertising identifier and never asks for tracking permission. In App Store terms: Data Not Collected.

3. Your cars and entries

Everything you enter — cars, plates, odometer readings, fill-ups, services, expenses, reminders, documents and notes — is stored on your device only, in the app’s own storage. A few settings, such as the units for new cars, are kept in the app’s preferences on the device. We can’t see, read or recover any of it.

Like other app data, your entries are included in your device backups if you use iCloud Backup or back up to your computer. Those backups belong to your Apple Account or your computer, and we have no access to them. Carsheet doesn’t sync your data between devices.

4. Camera, photos and receipts

When you attach a receipt or a paper, Carsheet uses the camera or reads only the photos and files you pick. iOS asks for permission the first time; you can change it in your device’s Settings. Receipt photos and PDFs are saved as files on your device, next to the app’s data, and never leave it unless you share them.

5. Reminders

Reminders before a service or a legal date are local notifications planned on your device; nothing is sent through a server. The app asks for permission the first time you save a reminder, and you can turn notifications off in Settings › Notifications in Carsheet or in your device’s Settings.

6. Widgets, Controls and Shortcuts

The widgets and the Control read your cars’ next due dates and last fill-ups from a file the app keeps in a shared folder on your device; that file never leaves the device. Shortcuts and Siri run the app’s actions on your device.

7. Backups, exports and imports

The backups Carsheet makes are files on your device. When you share a backup, export CSV files or export the record PDF, the file is created on your device and goes only where you send it with the share sheet (Mail, Messages, Files, AirDrop or another app). When you import a file, it is read on your device. Links to sources and to our website open in your browser only when you tap them. These transfers are handled by Apple or by the app you choose, not by us.

8. Ratings

Once, after your fifth entry, the app may show Apple’s standard rating prompt. Whether and how you rate is handled entirely by Apple; the app only keeps, on your device, how many entries you have made and whether it has asked.

9. Emails to support

If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you send, including the app version, iOS version, device, language, region and number of cars that the in-app support email adds. We use this only to answer you, don’t share it, and delete it when it is no longer needed for your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

10. Deleting your data

You can delete single entries, a car with its records, and backup versions in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data, receipt photos included, from your device. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request, apart from emails you have sent us.

11. Children

Carsheet is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you and to complain to a data protection authority. Since the app collects no data, these rights concern only emails you have sent us; write to ssx51f@gmail.com.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts to collect data, the policy will say so before that version is released.

14. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, write to ssx51f@gmail.com.
